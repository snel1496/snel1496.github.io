# What are Radiants?

This portion is for those who have not read the books and want an understanding of how the magic system that originates on Roshar works. A fair note, their will obviously be **minor spoilers** for the books.

Becoming a Radiant requires swearing yourself to an ideal, similar to a paladin in D&D. The type of idea, and a bit of the personality of the person, will determine the radiant order. For example, someone who isn't very outgoing and possibly selfish **could not** become a [Windrunner](#windrunners), but they could be a [Lightweaver](#lightweavers)

When swearing this ideal the person is doing this with a spren, and that spren (the DM in this case) must accept those words. Which does mean that you can not just say the words, you must **feel** them, and have grown to be them.

## What Radiant Orders Are Available to Play?

There are 10 orders of Radiants, with 9 of them available to play. Bondsmiths are the only that are not available to actually play, although they are in the handbook for descriptive and world building purposes. If you want to know more about them you can find them on page 206 or [in the coppermind](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Bondsmiths)

## Dustbringers

Dustbringers are highly mobile, with a penchant for crafting and engineering. They also have a reputation for being thrill-seekers who dive headfirst into any situation. They are capable of the most destruction when it comes to the radiant orders, which is why they pride themselves on their control, precision, and understanding. 

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Investigator or Thief)
- Hunter (Archer, Assassin, or Tracker)
- Scholar (Artifabrian or Strategist)


If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 132 or within the [Order of Dustbringers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Dustbringers) page on the coppermind

## Edgedancers

Edgedancers come from all walks of life, though the must care more about service to and advocacy for the common folk than they do for impressing the powerful. Those most attracted to the order often grew up destitute or similarly understand what it is to struggle, and religious practitioners, doctors, and other civil servants often find themselves attracted to the order. They tend to ignore high society and focus on listening to those in need, even listening to their enemies, as their stories could provide insight

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Thief)
- Envoy (Diplomat, Faithful, or Mentor)
- Hunter (Tracker)
- Leader (Champion)
- Scholar (Surgeon)


If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 140 or within the [Order of Edgedancers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Edgedancers) page on the coppermind

## Elsecallers

While the Order of Elsecallers accepts all who genuinely wish to improve themselves, the order's combination of surges is particularly powerful in the hands of a quick and strategic mind. It also appeals to those who wish to aid others in journeys of self-improvement. Thoughtful, careful, and cautious, they are typically viewed as the wisest of the Radiants.

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Investigator)
- Envoy (Faithful or Mentor)
- Leader (Officer)
- Scholar (Stategist or Surgeon)
- Warrior (Soldier)


If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 148 or within the [Order of Elsecallers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Elsecallers) page on the coppermind

## Lightweavers

The Lightweavers attract personalities of all types, from the quiet and introspective to the gregarious and outgoing, so long as they have an artistic spirit. The order is also of particular interest to those with a penchant for subterfuge. Almost all lightweavers are artists at heart, regardless of their personal abilities.

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Investigator, Spy, Thief)
- Envoy (Diplomat)
- Hunter (Assassin)
- Leader (Politico)
- Scholar (Strategist)

If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 156 or within the [Order of Lightweavers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Lightweavers) page on the coppermind

## Skybreakers

Skybreakers are investigators, law enforcement officers, attorneys, and litigators. Some focus on the physical aspects of law enforcement, while others become experts in the legal codes of nations throughout Roshar. Their highest purpose is to follow the law and to ensure it serves everyone well.

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Investigator or Spy)
- Envoy (Diplomat)
- Hunter (Archer or Assassin)
- Scholar (Strategist)
- Warrior (Duelist)

If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 164 or within the [Order of Skybreakers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Skybreakers) page on the coppermind

## Stonewards

Stonewards are known for their teamwork, dependability, and love of a good challenge. People who enjoy sport, martial arts, or wilderness exploration easily find themselves drawn to the Stonewards. It is not uncommon to find them putting other peoples interests before their own. They are the rock to lean on.

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Envoy (Mentor)
- Hunter (Tracker)
- Leader (Champion or Officer)
- Warrior (Duelist, Sharbearer, or Soldier)

If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 172 or within the [Order of Stonewards](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Stonewards) page on the coppermind

## Truthwatchers

Truthwatchers seek truth wherever it can be found: artifabrians, scientists, philosophers, journalists, and all manner of scholars and thinkers often find their way to this order. Their ultimate goal is to find truths and share them.

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Investigator or Thief)
- Envoy (Diplomat or Faithful)
- Scholar (Artifabrian, Strategist, Surgeon)

If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 180 or within the [Order of Truthwatchers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Truthwatchers) page on the coppermind

## Willshapers

Willshapers are freedom fighters, teachers, and leaders who all work in their own ways to free the oppressed and create opportunities for others to express themselves. Even before bonding lightspren, Willshapers are often architects, crafters, and civil engineers. They are possibly the most nature radiant order for Singers to follow. 

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Envoy (Mentor)
- Leader (Champion, Officer, or Politico)
- Scholar (Artifabrian or Surgeon)
- Warrior (Soldier)

If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 190 or within the [Order of Willshapers](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Willshapers) page on the coppermind

## Windrunners 

Though the Windrunners heavily emphasize excellence in weaponry, their capabilities also make them excellent scouts, and their history emphasizes leadership. They often will be found protecting those in need.

**Recommended Heroic Paths**
- Agent (Spy)
- Envoy (Mentor)
- Hunter (Archer)
- Leader (Champion or Officer)
- Warrior (Duelist or Soldier)

If you want to know more they can be found in the handbook at page 198 or within the [Order of Windrunners](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Order_of_Windrunners) page on the coppermind
