    - 16 oz Shredded or cubbed Tofu

    - 2 cloves garlic minced

    - black pepper
    - 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese shredded
    - block of low-fat cream cheese

# Description

In my opinion, this is an incredible iteration of the classic Broccoli Cheddar Soup. The original source is here: https://www.eatingonadime.com/broccoli-cheese-soup/. This can be made in a crock pot, just add all ingrediants minus the cheeses and cook on low for for 6-8 hours, adding the cheeses an hour or two before eatting.

I also love to get an interesting take on cheddar cheese, last time I made this I used a spicy soft cheddar that added some dimension.

# Steps to Create
1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook until softened.
3. Stir in the flour and cook until combined.
4. Slowly whisk in the chicken broth and heavy whipping cream until smooth.
5. Add the broccoli, carrots, celary, tofu, sausage, (anything else you want, go crazy). Stir to combine.
6. Bring the soup to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and cook for 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender.
7. Stir in the shredded cheddar cheese until completely melted and smooth.
8. Serve warm and enjoy.
